## How to Use This Checklist

1. **On walks**, use this as a "what to look for" reference — many of these may already be on the 50 acres
2. **As species are identified**, update the "On Property?" column
3. **When filling niches** in the food forest design, scan this list for PNW-adapted candidates
4. **Cross-reference** with the species database for ecological function data
5. **Bolded species** are high-priority: either already documented or key indigenous food plants
6. **Source URL** for each species: `northernbushcraft.com/plants/[name]/notes.htm`

## Priority Species for Field Verification

Based on habitat suitability and indigenous food traditions:

### High Priority - Look for on walks:
- **Camas / Blue Camas** - KEY indigenous food, design candidate
- **Black Huckleberry** - Fire-managed, highly productive
- **Stinging Nettle** - Multi-use (food, fiber, nutrient accumulator)
- **Saskatoon Berry / Serviceberry** - Excellent food forest candidate
- **Thimbleberry** - Common in PNW forests
- **Wild Rose** - Food, medicine, wildlife
- **Dandelion** - Dynamic accumulator, edible
- **Miner's Lettuce** - Common spring green

### Moderate Priority - Likely present:
- Chickweed, Clover, Violet, Plantain, Dock
- Wild Strawberry (already confirmed)
- Devil's Club (medicinal, habitat-appropriate)

### Design Candidates (not yet present, consider adding):
- Camas (root layer)
- Saskatoon Berry (understory)
- Gooseberry/Currant (shrub layer)
- Jerusalem Artichoke (root layer)
