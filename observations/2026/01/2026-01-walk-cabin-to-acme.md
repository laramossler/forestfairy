# Field Observation — January 2026 (Walk: Cabin to Acme Road)

**Time**: Afternoon, sunny day
**Weather**: Clear, sunny — rare winter sun
**Route/Area**: Main cabin down to Acme Road, along driveway and creek corridor

## Observations

### Flora
- Abundant lichen on branches throughout route — especially dense on north-facing slopes
- Oregon grape present along understory
- Sword ferns visible as evergreen groundcover
- Grand fir near cabin supporting Brown Creeper activity
- Dense canopy on slope towards stream blocks most sunlight

### Fauna

#### Birds Heard
- Red-breasted Nuthatch
- Song Sparrow
- Chestnut-backed Chickadee
- Golden-crowned Kinglet

#### Birds Previously Seen (Earlier in January)
- Great Horned Owl pair — 30-min duet at 9pm near cabin
- Northern Flicker — building nest
- Brown Creeper flock — on grand fir near cabin window
- Ruffed Grouse — twice along creek corridor

#### Mammals
- Douglas Squirrel (Chickaree) — regular visitor to deck
- Owl pellet found on forest floor (Great Horned or Barred Owl)
- Animal tracks in snow (possible grouse, deer, or other)

### Water
- Creek gurgling — good winter flow
- Healthy riparian zone with dense vegetation along banks

### Soil & Ground
- Snow persisting on north-facing slope toward stream
- Colder microclimate where sun doesn't penetrate canopy

### Microclimate Notes
- North-facing slope toward creek: colder, snow retention, less sun penetration through canopy
- Shade-loving species likely thrive here: sword fern, vanilla leaf, inside-out flower
- Creek corridor creates distinct moist microhabitat
- View of Mt. Hood visible through canopy gaps

## Reflections
First proper winter walk. The snow stays on the backside of the slope towards the stream — colder since the sun doesn't get through much of the canopy. The creek was just gurgling. Found what appears to be an owl pellet and possible grouse tracks in snow. The lichen coverage is remarkable — clean air indicator. Beautiful view of Mt. Hood through the trees.
